Partnerships

Partner with TalentGate

We are building a trusted network of partners across Australia and overseas — people and organisations who connect good employers with reliable workers. If that sounds like you, we would like to hear from you.

Who we partner with

Referral partners

Individuals and organisations — here or overseas — who personally know employers needing workers, or workers seeking genuine opportunities, and can refer them to us with their consent.

Community & industry partners

Community groups, training providers, industry associations and service organisations whose people we can support with recruitment, relocation and settlement services.

What a good partnership looks like

Our best partners share one habit: they refer people they actually know. A strong referral tells us how you know the employer or worker, what they genuinely need, and confirms they have agreed to be contacted. Quality always beats volume — one well-prepared referral is worth more than twenty names from a list.

Every referral you make is recorded to your own partner code, with date and time, under your agreement with us — so your work is always visible and always yours.

How it works

  1. Reach out. Email our General Manager with a few lines about who you are and the network you bring.
  2. A conversation. We will talk through how you work, the people you can reach, and whether we are the right fit for each other.
  3. Agreement & your partner code. We formalise the partnership in writing — including commercial terms — and issue your unique partner code.
  4. Your private referral link. You receive your own referral link. Every lead you lodge through it is recorded to your code and followed up within one business day.
